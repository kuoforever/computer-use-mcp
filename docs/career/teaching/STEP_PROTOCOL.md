# Step protocol

> **Status: current module of teaching protocol v1.**

## Before a non-trivial step

State the objective, required concepts, why it is next under the canonical
tracker, expected evidence, and what a failure would mean. This prediction is
made before seeing the result and prevents post-hoc storytelling.

## During execution

Explain consequential code paths, state transitions, command flags, design
alternatives, safety/privacy/compatibility trade-offs, and observations that
change the diagnosis. Summarize repeated edits, installs, formatting, polling,
and other mechanical work.

## After the step

Report the observed result, what it proves, what it does not prove, relevant
failure modes or alternatives, one likely interview question, and whether the
result is durable enough to enter [resume evidence](../resume/).

## Compact templates

> Before: Objective: ... Concept: ... Why now: ... Expected evidence: ...
> Failure means: ...

> After: Result: ... Proves: ... Does not prove: ... Interview angle: ...
> Next action: ...

These are structures, not mandatory literal wording. Keep simple steps simple.
